## Design Context

### Users
Chat Bridge serves Mac users who want to continue their desktop Agent conversations through messaging channels. Settings should make the active Agent, the single menu bar entry, and connection readiness easy to understand.

### Brand Personality
Calm, approachable, precise. Use concise Chinese interface copy and honest connection states.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the user's Hoy screenshots: a softly translucent cool-gray settings surface, a compact heading, rounded capsule controls with white selected states, generous but purposeful spacing, and fine section dividers. Keep the native macOS system typography and Agent brand assets. Avoid the oversized app heading, heavy toolbar, and crowded rectangular action buttons shown in the previous Chat Bridge settings.

The default window is a two-pane conversation workspace: searchable Agent cards on the left, a blue selected card, and a separate white message surface with a composer on the right. Settings open only through the settings entry, as a sheet over the workspace. Do not substitute settings for the home screen or fill unavailable conversations with invented messages.

The menu bar opens a separate floating conversation with no title or toolbar row. Follow the latest Hoy reference: a very subtle frosted backdrop with feathered edges, gray user bubbles aligned right, and milky white AI replies aligned left. The composer and cards retain soft shadows. Both the floating conversation and dashboard render Markdown; wide tables and code blocks scroll horizontally inside their bubbles. Cards must remain distinct over pale windows; fine white outlines alone are insufficient. Messages blur and fade near the top edge; the sides and bottom must also fade without a visible rectangular viewport or clipped shadows. Gaps remain scrollable and the conversation's vertical scroll indicators stay hidden.

The floating conversation has one quiet circular arrow button at its top right to open the dashboard. Opening the dashboard shows the Chat Bridge Dock icon and preserves the selected conversation and draft. Closing the dashboard returns to menu bar mode; it does not quit the service.

### Design Principles
1. Use one continuous settings surface; organize related rows with spacing and subtle dividers.
2. Give labels, supporting copy, and secondary actions clearly different emphasis.
3. Align controls consistently and use rounded, quiet treatments for secondary actions.
4. Preserve truthful availability states and existing preference behavior.
5. Support light and dark appearance, reduced transparency, keyboard navigation, and smaller windows.

### Brand and menu bar update · 2026-09-21

The app identity is a calm black two-eye bridge creature with a small chat tail, on a cornflower blue app-icon tile with restrained soft depth. The menu bar uses one monochrome template version, never an expanding Agent avatar group. Clicking it restores the last conversation and draft; Agent selection remains in the dashboard and settings. Product documentation leads with use cases and messaging, with English as the default and Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French and Spanish translations. Product demo media defaults to English, including interface labels, captions, messages and receipts. Demo media uses explicitly labelled fictional conversations.
